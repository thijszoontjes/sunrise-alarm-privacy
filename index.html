Sunrise Alarm

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 11 September 2026

Sunrise Alarm is a local-first alarm app published by Lunava Software. This policy explains what the current app does with information on your device and what may be processed by third-party services.

Prototype status: the current build is still under development. The publisher's final legal registration details and postal address will be added before a public production release. Privacy questions can already be sent to lunavasoftware@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores alarm times, repeat days, selected sounds, theme choices, sleep-session dates, derived sleep metrics and sleep-tracking settings locally on your device. The current app has no Sunrise Alarm account, backend, analytics service or cloud sleep database.

Local data is not uploaded by Sunrise Alarm. You can delete it in the app's Settings, reset the app or uninstall the app. Device backups and operating-system migration may retain app data according to your device settings.

2. Microphone and sleep tracking

Sleep Tracking is optional. When enabled and permitted, Sleep Mode processes live microphone samples locally on the device to estimate breathing rate, signal quality and experimental sleep metrics.

Raw microphone audio is processed in memory and is not stored or uploaded by Sunrise Alarm. Noise, movement, pets, other people, phone placement and device settings can make the estimates inaccurate. Sleep Tracking is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis or medical decisions.

3. Advertising

The current development configuration uses Google test ads and requests non-personalized ads. When Google Mobile Ads is enabled, Google may process information such as an IP address used to estimate general location, app interactions, diagnostics, app-performance information and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes.

Google processes information under its own policies and terms. The final production configuration must include the applicable consent flow and matching Play Console disclosures.

See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's processing details.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications support alarm reminders and alarm delivery.
  • Exact-alarm access supports user-created wake-up alarms on supported Android versions.
  • Microphone access is requested only for optional local Sleep Tracking.
  • Brightness, wake-lock and orientation access support the Sleep Mode experience.

You can deny or withdraw permissions in system settings, but the related feature may not work.

5. Purchases and third parties

The current prototype does not connect Sunrise Alarm to a cloud account or payment backend. If store purchases are enabled in a future release, Google Play or Apple will process payment and Sunrise Alarm will store only the entitlement needed to unlock the purchased feature; the app will not receive or store your card number.

6. Children

Sunrise Alarm is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact lunavasoftware@gmail.com.

7. Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Email lunavasoftware@gmail.com for privacy questions, deletion help or complaints. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

8. Changes

This policy may change when features, advertising partners or store integrations change. The date at the top identifies the current version.
